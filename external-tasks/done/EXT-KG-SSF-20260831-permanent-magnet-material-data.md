# Completion

The R2 material-data boundaries are now reflected in MAG-004/005.

- Material-family properties are separated from grade/application design limits.
- Ferrite, AlNiCo, SmCo and NdFeB are described qualitatively with the supplied scientifically stable distinctions.
- SmCo5 / Sm2Co17 are explicitly treated as subfamilies rather than one universal value set.
- NdFeB corrosion protection is described as frequently required for sintered grades, not universally mandatory.
- Curie temperature and working-temperature limits are separated.
- No timeless USD/kg values, universal Tmax values or fixed market rankings are taught.
- Market, production, export-control and recycling-capacity values are explicitly governed as dated evidence.
- Recycling capability is separated from actual end-of-life recovery and industrial capacity in MAG-006.
