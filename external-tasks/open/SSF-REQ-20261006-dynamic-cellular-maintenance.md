# SSF Request: Dynamic Cellular Maintenance learning path

Origin: research intake, 2026-10-06
Dependency: KG mappings
Priority: medium-high

Create a learning path from cellular damage to maintenance and organismal aging:
damage/protein turnover -> autophagy and lysosomes -> mitophagy/mitochondrial quality -> proteostasis -> senescence and immune clearance -> tissue/organ function.

Teach why gene expression is not the same as functional flux, why pathway activity can reflect either efficient maintenance or increased damage burden, and why effects can differ by cell type, sex, age and context.

Include the current human autophagy-flux and CMA/senescence studies as case studies with explicit evidence levels. Avoid a simplistic “boost autophagy” health message.
