# SSF Request: Biological Robustness through the Tardigrade case

Origin: BRE research strand
Priority: medium

Create a learning path using tardigrades to teach the difference between:
damage prevention, damage tolerance, repair, clearance and recovery.

Correct the "one supergene makes humans radiation-proof" narrative. Show how Dsup experiments differ from whole-organism tardigrade biology and from demonstrated human interventions.

Connect molecular mechanisms to radiation biology, oxidative stress, DNA repair, proteostasis and spaceflight. Include evidence grading and historical dates so old findings recirculated in current media are not mistaken for new discoveries.
