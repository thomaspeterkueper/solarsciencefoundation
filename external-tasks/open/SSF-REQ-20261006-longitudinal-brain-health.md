# SSF Request: Longitudinal Brain Health learning path

Origin: research synthesis, 2026-10-06
Dependency: KG mappings
Priority: high

Build a life-course learning path for brain health. Explain interacting domains rather than presenting a single anti-dementia checklist: education/cognitive reserve, sensory function, vascular/metabolic health, movement, smoking/alcohol, mood, social connection, environmental exposure and relevant emerging biological/longitudinal markers.

Use the Lancet 14-factor model and WHO 2026 multidomain guidance as evidence anchors. Teach the difference between population attributable risk and individual risk, association and intervention evidence, and risk reduction versus guaranteed prevention.

Connect existing Voice & Language and Dynamic Cellular Maintenance learning material as observation/mechanism layers.
