# SSF Request: Voice & Language biomarker learning/research path

**Origin:** research intake, 2026-10-06
**Dependency:** KG canonical mappings
**Priority:** medium-high

## Requested work
Build a learning path explaining how everyday speech can carry multiple kinds of information:
voice acoustics -> speech timing/prosody -> linguistic production -> derived speech clocks -> comparison with independent aging/cognition measures.

Teach explicitly:
- population model vs individual longitudinal baseline,
- correlation vs prediction vs diagnosis,
- confounding by language/culture/task/hearing/illness/recording conditions,
- why multimodal combinations can outperform single speech features,
- why validation across populations and longitudinal follow-up are required.

Use Hernandez et al. (Science Advances 2026, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aef9864) as a current case study, not as a clinical diagnostic recipe.
